# South Korea: heat deaths concentrated among the elderly and outdoor workers

## The numbers

South Korea's Korea Disease Control and Prevention Agency (KDCA) has recorded **238 heat-related deaths since monitoring began in 2011**. Of those, **156 — over 65% — were aged 60 or older** ([Korea heat deaths, elderly](../sources/korea-heat-deaths-elderly.md)).

In **2025, as of July 8, seven deaths** had been recorded, **four of them aged 70 or older** (same source).

## Where it happens

- **32%** of deaths occurred on farms
- **15%** at home
- roughly **14%** near roads

([Korea heat deaths, elderly](../sources/korea-heat-deaths-elderly.md))

The article names two cases directly: a woman in her 80s found unconscious near a greenhouse in Jinju, and another woman in her 80s found dead in a rice paddy on Jeju Island. Both were elderly women doing agricultural work alone — which the source calls "the shape of this problem in Korea."

## The official advice

Health authorities "recommended agriculture workers to work in early mornings or late evenings and avoid going outdoors in the hours between noon and 5 p.m." ([Korea heat deaths, elderly](../sources/korea-heat-deaths-elderly.md)).

**No dedicated cooling-center or officer-level program appears in our sources for South Korea** — the only response documented so far is this work-hours advisory. That's a gap, not a finding; if a program exists, it isn't cited here yet.
