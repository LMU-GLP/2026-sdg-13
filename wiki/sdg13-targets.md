# SDG 13 (Climate Action): the official targets

The UN's targets and indicators for Goal 13, most relevant to neighborhood-level heat safety ([UN SDG 13 targets](../sources/un-sdg13-targets.md)):

- **Target 13.1** — "Strengthen resilience and adaptive capacity to climate-related hazards and natural disasters in all countries," tracked by indicator 13.1.1: "Number of deaths, missing persons and directly affected persons attributed to disasters per 100,000 population."
- **Target 13.2** — "Integrate climate change measures into national policies, strategies and planning," tracked partly via total greenhouse gas emissions per year.
- **Target 13.3** — "Improve education, awareness-raising and human and institutional capacity on climate change mitigation, adaptation, impact reduction and early warning."
- **Target 13.a** — Deliver the **$100 billion annual** climate finance commitment; COP29 raised the ask to **$1.3 trillion annually by 2035** for developing countries.
- **Target 13.b** — Build capacity for least developed countries and small island developing states.

Global context the UN page reports: 2025 temperatures were **1.43°C above pre-industrial levels**; CO₂ was **152% above pre-industrial levels** in 2024; disaster mortality fell **65%** over the past decade; **141 countries** now have disaster risk reduction strategies (all: [UN SDG 13 targets](../sources/un-sdg13-targets.md)).

Indicator 13.1.1 — deaths and disaster-affected persons per 100,000 population — is the direct thread connecting this global goal to the county- and city-level heat death data in [Los Angeles](los-angeles-heat.md) and [South Korea](south-korea-heat.md).
