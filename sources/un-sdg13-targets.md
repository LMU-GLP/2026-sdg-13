# SDG 13: Climate Action — official targets and indicators

- **Source:** United Nations, Sustainable Development Goals
- **URL:** https://sdgs.un.org/goals/goal13
- **Read on:** 2026-07-31

## Key figures and targets

- **Target 13.1:** "Strengthen resilience and adaptive capacity to climate-related hazards and natural disasters in all countries." Measured in part by indicator 13.1.1, "Number of deaths, missing persons and directly affected persons attributed to disasters per 100,000 population."
- **Target 13.2:** "Integrate climate change measures into national policies, strategies and planning," tracked via indicator 13.2.2, "Total greenhouse gas emissions per year."
- **Target 13.3:** "Improve education, awareness-raising and human and institutional capacity on climate change mitigation, adaptation, impact reduction and early warning."
- **Target 13.a:** Implement the **$100 billion annual** climate finance commitment; COP29 set a new goal of **$1.3 trillion annually by 2035** for developing countries.
- **Target 13.b:** Promote capacity-building mechanisms for least developed countries and small island developing states.
- Global temperatures in 2025 were **"1.43°C above pre-industrial levels."**
- CO₂ concentrations reached **"152% above pre-industrial levels"** in 2024.
- Disaster-related mortality declined **65%** over the past decade; **141 countries** had adopted national disaster risk reduction strategies by 2025.

## Summary

SDG 13 sets targets on disaster resilience, climate policy integration, public education, and climate finance, with indicator 13.1.1 (deaths and disaster-affected persons per 100,000 population) as the direct link to neighborhood-level heat safety. The UN's own tracking shows both progress (65% drop in disaster mortality over a decade, 141 countries with risk reduction strategies) and a widening finance gap (from $100 billion to a stated need of $1.3 trillion a year).
