# One person dies from extreme heat every minute worldwide: Lancet Countdown 2025

- **Source:** *Business Standard*, October 29, 2025, reporting on the 2025 Lancet Countdown on Health and Climate Change (128 experts, 71 institutions, 57 indicators)
- **URL:** https://www.business-standard.com/health/heat-related-deaths-lancet-countdown-2025-study-125102900182_1.html
- **Read on:** 2026-07-31

## Key figures

- **Nearly 550,000 people** are dying from heat every year, worldwide.
- That is a **63 per cent jump since the 1990s.**
- **Adults over 65 face a threefold rise** in heatwave days compared with the 1990s.
- **Babies under one year** now face **nearly four times as many heatwave days** as in the 1990s.
- **84 per cent** of the heatwave days people experienced between 2020 and 2024 "would not have occurred without human-induced climate change."

## ⚠️ Do not average or combine with LA/Korea counts

This is a global estimate for a different population (worldwide, 2012–2021 method) than either the LA County brief (recorded county deaths, 2015–2024) or the Korea KDCA figures (national heat-illness deaths since 2011). All three use different methods and scopes; none should be added together or presented as the same measurement.

## Summary

The 2025 Lancet Countdown puts the global toll of heat at nearly 550,000 deaths a year, up 63% since the 1990s, and finds people over 65 are experiencing three times as many heatwave days as in the 1990s. It attributes 84% of recent (2020–2024) heatwave days directly to human-caused climate change, tying the neighborhood-level heat problem to a documented global trend.
